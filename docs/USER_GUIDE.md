## Getting Started & Roles

Nexus CRM supports multiple roles within a Merchant account:
- **Admin**: Full access. Can manage subscriptions, configure system settings, manage the full product catalog, and view analytics.
- **Agent**: Operational access. Can handle customer conversations in the Inbox, draft orders, and update shipment statuses.

### Onboarding
When you first log in, you will be taken to your Dashboard. From there, you can navigate to Products to set up your inventory, or Inbox to start replying to customers.

## Subscription Tiers
Nexus CRM offers three subscription tiers. You can manage your tier in the `/subscription` page.
- **Starter**: Perfect for new businesses. Includes up to 500 orders/month, 1 channel integration, and basic analytics.
- **Pro**: For growing teams. Includes unlimited orders, all channel integrations, full product/stock management, and advanced analytics.
- **Ultimate**: The complete package. Includes everything in Pro, plus our **AI Chat Extraction** (Zero-Touch extraction), priority Inbox sorting, and marketing broadcasts.

## Inbox & Chat Management
Your Inbox is where all customer conversations from Meta (Instagram, Facebook Messenger) and WhatsApp live.
- **Real-Time Updates**: The Inbox updates automatically when new messages arrive.
- **View Chat**: You can always view the full context of a conversation directly linked to any order.
- **AI Extraction (Ultimate Tier)**: If you are on the Ultimate tier, our AI automatically reads incoming chat texts and extracts the customer's Name, Phone Number, City, Street Address, and desired Product. It pre-fills the "Smart Order" drawer for one-click order drafting.

## Order Management Workflow
Nexus CRM is optimized for the Cash-on-Delivery (COD) model common in the Balkan market.

### Creating Orders
1. **Manual Draft**: You can manually create an order from the Inbox or Orders page by selecting products and entering customer details.
2. **Smart Draft (AI)**: Click on an AI-extracted suggestion in the chat to automatically create a draft order.

### Order Statuses
Orders move through the following lifecycle:
- **Draft**: Initial creation. Stock is not yet affected.
- **Confirmed**: Order is ready to be processed.
- **Dispatched**: Order has been given to the courier.
- **Delivered**: Order reached the customer. **Stock is automatically decremented.**
- **Returned / Cancelled**: Order failed or was returned. **Stock is incremented back** to your inventory.

### Invoices
You can easily generate, save, and print professional PDF invoices directly from the Order view to ensure a seamless post-order experience.

## Inventory & Product Management
Manage your catalog in the Products tab.
- **Images**: Upload images for your products. Thumbnails will appear in the inventory list and order summaries.
- **Stock Tracking**: Keep track of your available inventory. Stock levels are automatically managed as orders move to "Delivered" or "Returned".

## Analytics & Reporting
The Dashboard provides key insights into your business:
- **Active Chats**: See how many conversations need attention.
- **COD Revenue**: Calculate your pending and collected Cash-on-Delivery revenue.
- **City Demographics**: Nexus standardizes city inputs via searchable dropdowns to ensure accurate geographical reporting and data integrity.
